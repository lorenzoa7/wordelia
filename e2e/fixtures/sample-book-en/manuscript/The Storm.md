title: The Storm
---
Elias lit the lighthouse lamp at six o’clock sharp, as he had done for twenty-two years. Outside, the sea was very rough, and the waves struck the rocks with an old, almost personal anger. He looked at the horizon and then felt that something was different that night.

The wind came in through the cracks of the window and made the flame shiver inside the glass. Elias counted the boats in the harbor, one by one, and saw that the *Swallow* was missing.

“Did you feel that?” Marta asked, climbing the last steps with a mug of coffee in her hands.

“I did,” he said, without taking his eyes off the sea. “The Swallow hasn’t come back yet.”

Marta set the mug on the table and came to the window. For a moment the two of them stood in silence, listening to the light turn above their heads, a groan of iron that neither of them noticed anymore.

* * *

At eleven that night, the beam of the lighthouse found a boat. It was not the Swallow. It was smaller, painted a blue that nobody on the island used, and it carried a single man, lying in the bottom with his eyes open to the rain.
