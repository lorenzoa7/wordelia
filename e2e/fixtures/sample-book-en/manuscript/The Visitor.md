title: The Visitor
---
The man from the blue boat slept for two days. Marta changed the cloths on his forehead and wrote down in a notebook everything he said in his fever: names of streets that did not exist on the island, a date, and a word she did not know.

“He said it again,” Marta told Elias when he came into the room. “The same word. *Aurora.*”

Elias took off his wet hat and sat on the edge of the bed. The visitor had the hands of someone who had never worked at sea, and a silver ring with a lighthouse engraved on it.
